Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MedVeda ("we", "our", "the app") is a study platform for BAMS (Ayurvedic Medicine) students. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us about it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and mobile number when you register. Your mobile number can also be used as an alternative login identifier instead of your email address.
  • Usage data: which notes, videos and quizzes you access, and your quiz scores, to show your own progress and download history.
  • Payment information: when you purchase notes, payment is processed by Razorpay. MedVeda does not store your card, UPI ID, or bank details — only the payment status and a Razorpay-issued payment ID are recorded on our servers.
  • Device information: a push-notification token, if you enable notifications, so we can send you study reminders and updates.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and unlock content you've purchased.
  • To process payments securely via Razorpay.
  • To send optional study reminders and app notifications.
  • To respond to support requests sent to medvedaforu@gmail.com.

3. How we store your information

Data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL database, hosted in the ap-south-1 / Mumbai region) with row-level security so that a student can only ever access their own account data and content they've purchased.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with Razorpay (to process a purchase) and Supabase (our database/hosting provider), both solely to operate the app.

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from inside the app (Profile → Delete Account), or by emailing medvedaforu@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile, purchase history and quiz scores from our systems.

6. Children's privacy

MedVeda is intended for BAMS students (typically 17 years and older) and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Contact us

For any privacy questions, email medvedaforu@gmail.com.
