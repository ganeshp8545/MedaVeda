How it works

How MedVeda works

From download to your first chapter.

  1. Download & register

    Install MedVeda from Google Play. Register with your full name, email address, mobile number and a password (optionally add a friend's referral code).

  2. Verify your email

    We email you a verification link. Tap it and you land on a page confirming your account is active. Then sign in with your email or mobile number.

  3. Select course & year

    Choose your course (such as BAMS) and academic year. This decides what appears on Home, Subjects, Store and Search.

  4. Study

    Open a subject, then a chapter, and use its notes, video lectures and MCQ tests.

  5. Unlock premium notes

    Buy notes from a chapter or the Store. Payment happens in the app via Razorpay and access unlocks after it succeeds.

Free vs premium

Free

Video lectures

Every video lecture is free for every student.

Free

MCQ tests

Every test is free to attempt, with scores and stats.

Premium

PDF notes

Notes are sold as paid packages. A few individual notes may be marked free.

How purchases unlock content

Notes packages come in four sizes: chapter, subject, academic year and multi-year bundle. Buying a bigger package covers everything inside it. You will see the options that apply to the chapter you are viewing. When payment is confirmed, the notes unlock and appear under My Purchases.

If a payment goes wrong, see the payments FAQ and the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How access is tied to course and year

What you see follows the course and year on your profile. Purchases belong to the course of the product you bought. Change your selection any time from Profile → Change Course / Year.

Using MedVeda offline

Download notes you can access, and quizzes, to your device from inside the app. They then open from the Downloads screen without internet. Downloads can be paused, resumed and removed. Video lectures need a connection. Quiz scores taken offline are saved on your device and sync when you reconnect.
