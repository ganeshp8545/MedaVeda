FAQ

Quick answers

What is MedVeda?

MedVeda is a mobile study app for AYUSH degree students. For your course and academic year it organises study material as subject → chapter, with PDF notes, video lectures and MCQ tests.

Which courses are supported?

BAMS, BNYS, BSMS, BUMS and BHMS are set up on the platform. Courses are managed by the MedVeda team, so more can be added over time. What you can study depends on the content published for your course and year.

Is MedVeda free?

Video lectures and MCQ tests are free. PDF notes are sold as paid packages (a few individual notes may be marked free). See How it works.

How do I create an account?

Open the app, tap Register and enter your full name, email address, mobile number and a password. You will then pick your course and academic year.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and enter your email or mobile number. Open the reset email and tap the link. You can set a new password on our secure reset page, or choose "Reset inside the app". If the link has expired, request a new one from the app.

How do I buy notes?

Open a chapter, subject or the Store and choose an option: notes can be sold per chapter, per subject, per academic year, or as a multi-year bundle. Pay in the app through Razorpay. When payment succeeds the notes unlock.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. You can also follow the steps on our Account & Data Deletion page.
