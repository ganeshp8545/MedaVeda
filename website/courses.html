How content is organised

BAMS content follows this structure.

  1. Course

    You choose your course when you start, for example BAMS.

  2. Academic year

    Each course has its own academic years. Choose the year you are studying.

  3. Subject

    Every year contains its subjects.

  4. Chapter

    Each subject is split into chapters, each with notes, videos and MCQ tests.
