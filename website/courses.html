Courses on MedVeda

Five AYUSH degrees are set up on the platform, with more to come.

BAMS

Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery. A degree in Ayurveda, the traditional Indian system of medicine.

BNYS

Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences. A degree combining naturopathy with yoga therapy.

BSMS

Bachelor of Siddha Medicine and Surgery. A degree in the Siddha system of medicine.

BUMS

Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery. A degree in the Unani system of medicine.

BHMS

Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery. A degree in homoeopathic medicine.

More coming

Courses are managed by the MedVeda team, so the list can grow without an app update.

Descriptions are general and neutral. MedVeda is not affiliated with any university or regulatory council, and the availability of content depends on what has been published for each course. See the Disclaimer.

How content is organised

Every course follows the same simple structure.

  1. Course

    You choose your course when you start, for example BAMS.

  2. Academic year

    Each course has its own academic years. Choose the year you are studying.

  3. Subject

    Every year contains its subjects.

  4. Chapter

    Each subject is split into chapters, each with notes, videos and MCQ tests.

Find your course in the app

Select your course and year during sign-up. You can change them later from your profile.
