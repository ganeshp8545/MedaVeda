{{icon:layers}}

Course & academic-year content

Choose your course and academic year when you start. Browse all academic years within your course from Home, Subjects, Store and Search. Browsing another year leaves your profile year unchanged. Videos are free; paid notes require a purchase.

{{icon:book}}

Subjects, chapters, notes, videos and tests

Each academic year contains subjects; each subject is split into chapters; each chapter has its PDF notes, video lectures and MCQ tests, so everything for a topic sits in one place.

{{icon:file}}

Secure PDF notes viewer Premium

Read notes inside the app in a built-in PDF viewer. Paid notes are delivered through short-lived secure links and only to students who own them. A few notes may be marked free and can be opened without purchase.

{{icon:video}}

Video lectures Free

Watch chapter-wise video lectures. Videos are free for every student and open from the lecture's online link.

{{icon:quiz}}

Chapter & subject MCQ tests Free

Attempt MCQ tests with a 30-second timer per question, review earlier answers, and get a score at the end. Your attempts are saved to your account, and quiz stats show how you are doing. Tests are free.

{{icon:search}}

Global search with filters

Search across academic years, subjects, chapters, PDF notes, video lectures, MCQ tests, store products, your purchases and notifications. Narrow results with filters, and pick up your recent searches with one tap.

{{icon:resume}}

Continue learning & recently viewed

Home shows a Continue Learning card that reopens the last chapter you viewed, plus a list of recently viewed chapters. This history is stored on your device.

{{icon:download}}

Offline downloads

Download PDF notes and quizzes you have access to and open them from the Downloads screen with no connection. Downloads support pause, resume, retry and cancel, and you can remove them any time. Quiz scores completed offline sync when you reconnect.

{{icon:flame}}

Study streak

A streak badge counts the consecutive days you open the app. It is calculated on your device.

{{icon:clock}}

Study reminders

Set a daily study reminder at a time you choose. It is a local notification scheduled by your phone, so it works without internet.

{{icon:quote}}

Daily quote

A daily quote card on Home to keep you motivated.

{{icon:bell}}

Push notifications & preferences

Get notified about new notes, new videos, offers and discounts, payment updates, exam reminders and app updates. Choose exactly which categories you want in Notification Preferences, and see past messages in the in-app notifications list.

{{icon:cart}}

Store & payments Premium

Buy notes as a chapter, subject, academic-year or multi-year bundle package. The Google Play Android app uses Google Play Billing. Separate permitted distribution builds use Razorpay. MedVeda does not store your card or UPI details.

{{icon:receipt}}

My purchases & order history

See your orders and the content you have unlocked in one place.

{{icon:gift}}

Refer & Earn

When the referral programme is switched on, you get a personal referral code to share. Friends enter it at sign-up, and you can track each referral as pending, qualified or rewarded. Reward rules are set by MedVeda and shown in the app. If the programme is off, the screen tells you.

{{icon:user}}

Profile, course/year change & password

Edit your profile, change your course or year, change your password, and control notifications, all from Profile and Settings. Signing in works with your email or mobile number, and you can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in screen.

{{icon:shield}}

Screenshot protection

To protect paid content, MedVeda blocks screenshots and screen recording on supported devices.

{{icon:trash}}

Delete your account

Delete your account and data from Settings → Delete Account. See Account & Data Deletion.
