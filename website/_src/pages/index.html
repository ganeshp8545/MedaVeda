How it works

From sign-up to studying in minutes

  1. Create your account

  2. Select course & year

    Choose your degree and academic year. You can change it later.

  3. Study

    Open notes, watch videos and take MCQ tests chapter by chapter.

  4. Unlock premium notes

    Buy notes per chapter, subject, year or bundle and read them instantly.
