3. Why we use your data, and consent

We process your data to provide the Service you asked for (account, content, purchases), to run and secure it (fraud/abuse prevention, error monitoring), to communicate with you, and to comply with law. Where the DPDP Act requires consent, we rely on the consent you give when you register and use the app; you can withdraw consent by deleting your account (section 9), and you can switch off push notifications in the app or your phone settings at any time. We may also process data for "legitimate uses" the DPDP Act recognises, such as complying with legal obligations.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that process it for us:

  • Supabase – database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Our Supabase project is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the Asia-Pacific (Seoul, ap-northeast-2) region, so your data is stored and processed outside India.
  • Razorpay – payment processing. Razorpay receives the details needed to complete a payment and has its own privacy policy.
  • Expo (Expo Push Notification service) – delivers push notifications to your device using your push token.
  • Sentry – crash and error reporting, when enabled in the release build.
  • Google Play / Google – app distribution, Google Play Billing and purchase verification. This includes purchase identifiers and product IDs needed for those services. Google also provides Google Fonts on our website (see section 10).
  • Resend – sends our account emails (email verification and password reset) on our behalf; it is set up as the custom SMTP provider for our authentication service. Resend receives your email address and the content of those messages in order to deliver them.
  • Authorities – where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

They are used to detect repeat first-purchase discounts and referral abuse.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Supabase, row-level access rules so that students can read only their own account data, secure device storage for your session, time-limited signed links for paid notes, and restricted administrative access. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your password private. If a personal data breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Children

MedVeda is intended for students of degree-level AYUSH courses and is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please use the app only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children without verifiable parental consent; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or follow the steps on the Account & Data Deletion page.

10. Website, cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics scripts on it. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. The sign-up verification and password-reset pages read a secure token from the link you opened, use it only to talk to Supabase, and remove it from the address bar immediately; they do not store it in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage.
