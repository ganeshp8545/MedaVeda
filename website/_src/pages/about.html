About MedVeda

Learn · Heal · Grow.

Our mission

MedVeda is a digital learning platform built to make quality education more accessible, organised and engaging for learners. Our focus is students of AYUSH degrees: BAMS, BNYS, BSMS, BUMS and BHMS.

We bring subject-wise PDF notes, video lectures, MCQ tests, downloadable resources and progress tracking into one secure app, arranged by course, academic year, subject and chapter, so that self-study is simpler.

Our vision

To become a trusted and innovative learning platform by giving students reliable educational resources and modern technology, and to keep improving the experience with new features, performance work and updated content.

What we are and are not

MedVeda is a study aid. It is not a university, a regulator or a medical service, and it does not provide medical advice. Please read our Disclaimer.
