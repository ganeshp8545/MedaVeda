# MedVeda adult-audience privacy and Play review

**Working review, 2026-10-15. Not for publication or Console submission.** The operator intends MedVeda for adults and has explicitly decided to keep registration, login and authentication unchanged, without age verification, declarations, date-of-birth collection or age-based blocking. Source findings below describe this checkout, not deployed behavior. No production system, live legal page, database schema, account, or payment code was changed for this review. See [the unpublished policy draft](PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) for the preserved data disclosures.

**Later local update, 2026-10-15:** current release-document and website legal-page package labels were corrected to `in.medveda.app` in the shared working tree. The table below records the earlier audit snapshot. Runtime Play verification and store links still use `com.medveda.app`; see [the release report](MEDVEDA_PRIVACY_RELEASE_REPORT.md).

## 1. Intended audience and actual access

| Question | Source finding and limit |
|---|---|
| Age verification / declaration | Neither exists in `RegisterScreen.tsx`, `registerSchema.ts`, or `authService.ts`. The form asks for name, email, mobile, password, and optional referral code. |

| Actual access | There is no age gate to bypass: age is neither requested nor checked. Existing accounts and sessions are also not age-classified. |
| Website registration | Reviewed website source has marketing, verification and password-reset pages, but no account creation form. It directs visitors to register in the app. A live external or later website flow is unverified. |
| Existing accounts | The reviewed profile/signup code contains no birth date or age status. Existing users' ages cannot be inferred reliably from their course, email, payment method, or profile. Production records were not accessed. |
| Terms and notices | App Terms and website Terms expressly allow guardian-supervised use. Their privacy pages also allow under-18 use. These conflict with the new business decision and remain live/source unchanged pending approval. |
| Play audience | Current Play Console target-age choices and content-rating answers are inaccessible here. The repo's release documents also disagree on package name; `mobile-app/app.json` says `in.medveda.app`, while `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md` and website legal pages say `com.medveda.app`. |

**Business decision and open risk:** Do not add any age-related app, registration, authentication or backend control. The adult-audience rule is a business/eligibility statement, not an implemented age-verification mechanism. Minors may in practice register and use the service, and their ordinary account and activity data may be collected. Obtain legal advice on whether the intended-audience statement and current access pattern are supportable, what duties apply if a minor uses the service, and how credible reports should be handled. The operator must approve a documented review, notice, purchase/refund, account-access and data-handling process; do not infer users' ages from course, payment, or profile data, and do not restrict or delete existing accounts based on an assumption.

## 3. Google Play decision worksheet

- **Target audience:** **No age group is selected or recommended for submission yet.** First verify the current Console setting, actual app content, imagery, store listing, likely and observed audience, and the legal implications of minors being able to register. The owner's adult-audience intent alone does not establish the correct declaration. Google says target groups must reflect the audience the app is actually designed for and may consider child-directed imagery. Google's optional *Restrict Minor Access* feature can limit discovery/download/purchases for users it identifies as minors, but existing installs can continue to use the app; it is not proof that this app verifies age. Do not enable it or alter Console answers in this review. [Target audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159)
- **Families:** Do not select child groups merely because the app teaches students. If content or presentation actually targets children, reassess the audience and Families duties; an 18+ statement alone cannot settle this. Confirm applicable local-law definitions with counsel. [Families Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335)
- **User Data / deletion:** Google requires an in-app and external account-deletion request path for apps with account creation, plus accurate disclosure of any retained data. Existing paths are visible in source; complete cleanup and production verification remain open. [User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311), [deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)
- **Data Safety:** Prepare answers for all versions currently distributed under the actual Play package, including SDK behavior, provider sharing, purchase tokens, analytics/search, push tokens and conditional Sentry. Do not claim no collection. Console declarations are not verified here. [Data Safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)
- **Payments:** Digital notes in a Play-distributed app generally require Google Play Billing unless an eligible enrolled exception applies. The existing release gate reports no current alternative-billing eligibility; keep Play checkout Play-only. No Billing or Razorpay code was changed. [Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738)
