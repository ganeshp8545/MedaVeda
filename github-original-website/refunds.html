Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

MedVeda sells digital study notes that are unlocked instantly after a successful payment. Because access is granted immediately, refunds are limited to the cases below.

1. Eligible for a refund

  • Money was deducted from your account/UPI app but the notes were not unlocked in MedVeda within 24 hours of payment.
  • You were charged more than once for the same notes due to a technical error.
  • The notes you purchased are found to be significantly different from what was described in the app (e.g. wrong chapter/subject).

2. Not eligible for a refund

  • You changed your mind after the notes were successfully unlocked and accessed.
  • You purchased the wrong year/subject/chapter by mistake — please double-check before paying; contact support first and we'll try to help before you request a refund.

3. How to request a refund

Email medvedaforu@gmail.com with your registered email address, the product name, and the Razorpay payment ID (shown on the payment confirmation/failure screen in the app). We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

4. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank/UPI provider.

5. Failed / unconfirmed payments

If a payment is deducted but the app shows "Payment Failed" or the purchase never unlocks, no charge should remain — our system automatically reconciles failed/unconfirmed payments with Razorpay. If it doesn't resolve within 24 hours, contact support with your payment ID and we'll investigate manually.
