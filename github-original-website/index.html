Business & payment use case

A short summary of what MedVeda is and what the payment gateway is used for.

What MedVeda is

MedVeda is an education platform for BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) students in India. It's built as a mobile app (React Native/Expo) with a companion admin panel, backed by Supabase.

What is sold

The only paid product is digital study notes (PDF-style chapter/subject/year notes), delivered instantly and digitally inside the app after purchase. There is no physical shipping.

How payment is used

Razorpay is integrated via Supabase Edge Functions to process one-time UPI, card and netbanking payments for notes purchases, with signature verification and a webhook-based confirmation flow.

Who it's for

BAMS students across Indian Ayurvedic medical colleges preparing for university and college-level exams.

MV

MedVeda started from a simple frustration familiar to most BAMS students — notes scattered across WhatsApp groups, photocopies, and half-finished PDFs with no order to them. Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

The things students usually message us about.

Are the video lectures and quiz tests really free?
Yes. Every registered student gets full access to all video lectures and quiz tests at no cost — there's no trial period or watermark. The only thing you pay for is written notes, and only if you choose to buy them.
What if I buy the wrong chapter or subject?
Email us before you request a refund and we'll try to help directly. Once notes have been unlocked and accessed, they're generally not eligible for a refund — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for the exact cases that do qualify.
Can I study without an internet connection?
Yes. Once you've purchased notes, you can download them inside the app and read them offline anytime — no data connection needed after the download.
How do I pay, and is it safe?
Payments go through Razorpay via UPI, card, or netbanking. MedVeda never sees or stores your card, UPI ID, or bank details — only the payment status and a Razorpay-issued payment ID are recorded on our end.
Do I need an email address, or can I sign up with just my phone number?
Either works. You can register and log in with your email address or your 10-digit Indian mobile number, whichever's easier for you.
Is my personal data safe with MedVeda?
Your data is stored on Supabase, hosted in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region, with row-level security so you can only ever access your own account and purchases. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Pricing: Notes are priced individually per chapter/subject/year by the MedVeda team and shown in-app before purchase. Prices are typically in the ₹49–₹499 range depending on scope. Video lectures and quiz tests are free for all registered students.
